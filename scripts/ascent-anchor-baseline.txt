CatCrypt.Crypto.SecureCompilation.Ascent.AscendMeta	REACHABLE: proves keccakRoundBody_bitwise, a full Keccak-f[1600] round (theta/rho/pi/chi/iota) over 25 named lanes as a bitwise straight-line LowCT kernel, lifted to its BitVec 64 spec via 27 uses of its own ascend_meta tactic - a genuine kernel, not tactic scaffolding, with zero importers. No weld to Com jasminDialect/bytes yet; nearest template LegWeld.lean's legWeld_lower for a closed straight-line kernel with a source.
CatCrypt.Crypto.SecureCompilation.Ascent.CarryChainInterleave	INFRA: states the block tie for two independent carry chains threaded through CF and OF at once, lowering to adcx and adox. A property of the lowering, carrying no kernel to anchor. Its machine-side content is lowerAdcx_of and lowerAdox_cf, each reading JasminModel's x86_ADCX wrapper: adcx writes CF alone and adox OF alone, where adc goes through writeB5W and writes all five, which is why CarryChainTie admits no interleaving. On the dialect side the two chains are independent by construction, carries being SSA variables, so interCF_denote_eq_cfOnly is rfl and is about the block type rather than the hardware. Its witness is a seven-step alternating chain whose two carry sequences diverge at step 4, so cross-talk between the flags would change a reading. JInstr has no OF-clearing arm, so there is no clc analogue for OF and the OF carry-in is assumed rather than established.
CatCrypt.Crypto.SecureCompilation.Ascent.CarryChainTie	INFRA: states the block tie for a carry chain, relating the dialect's reg 1 carry variable to JState.flags.cf and lowering to adc. A property of the lowering rather than a claim about a kernel, so it carries none to anchor. Its witness is a two-limb addition, not an emitted artifact. Scope limits are in the module docstring: no interleaving with RegBlock arms, since lowerArith2 writes all five flags and would destroy CF between carry steps.
CatCrypt.Crypto.SecureCompilation.Ascent.CarryChainZeroIn	INFRA: establishes the carry-in CarryChainTie's tie assumes, by prefixing the chain with clc and proving CarryInterp holds afterwards from an arbitrary entry flag. A property of the lowering, carrying no kernel to anchor. Its witness is a two-limb addition run from a state whose CF is set, where the prefixed and unprefixed runs differ (12 against 13), which is what makes the prefix observable.
