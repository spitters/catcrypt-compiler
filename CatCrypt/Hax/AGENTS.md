# `CatCrypt/Hax/`: reification, typing and the ascent over hax-lean's `ImpExpr`

This directory relates hax-lean's `ImpExpr` to Lean functions in both directions:
the reifier `reifyImp%` turns a pure Lean function into an `ImpExpr`, and the ascent
in `Ascent/` generates shallow Lean functions with correspondence theorems from a
haxpipeT extraction. [HAX_RUST.md](../../HAX_RUST.md) gives both procedures.
Namespaces: `CatCrypt.Hax.*` for the top-level modules, `Hax.Ascent.*` for `Ascent/`.

## Conventions

- A new reified function: reify it, prove `FnCorres` with `ascend_reified`, add it to
  `CatCrypt.Hax.ReifyImp.pilots`, run `scripts/reify-roundtrip.sh`, and state the
  end-to-end theorem as `edwardsAdd_endToEnd` does.
- The `*Extracted` modules hold hax's reading of printed Rust as Lean literals; they
  are generated by the round-trip scripts.
- The translation from `ImpExpr` to LowCT lives in
  [`CatCrypt/Crypto/Hax/`](../Crypto/Hax/AGENTS.md).
