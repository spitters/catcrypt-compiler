## `ReprTransfer`

`CatCrypt/Crypto/Bridges/ReprTransfer.lean` and
`CatCrypt/Crypto/Bridges/ReprTransferExpr.lean` in this package carry the MIT
header "Copyright (c) 2026 CatCrypt Contributors". ParamTransfer, a required
dependency, contains the modules `ReprTransfer.lean` and `ReprTransferExpr.lean`
at its root under an LGPL-3.0 header ("Copyright (c) 2026 ParamTransfer
Contributors", "Authors: Bas Spitters"). The copies have the same line count and
declare the same structures and theorems (`ReprEmbedding`, `BinOpRealization`,
`BinOpRealization.eq_transfer`, `EndoOpRealization`, `denote_commutes`); they
differ in namespace (`CatCrypt.Crypto.Bridges.ReprTransfer` in this package,
`ReprTransfer` in ParamTransfer), in import lines, and in docstring wording. Both
modules entered the CatCrypt development history on 2026-06-18; ParamTransfer
added its copies in its initial commit on 2026-07-18. The package modules do not
import ParamTransfer's copies.
