BLS12-381 G1 multi-scalar multiplication — composed Pippenger vs blst (#487)
===========================================================================

Capstone of the BLS-vs-blst program: the three landed legs assembled into a
full G1 MSM and benched head-to-head against blst's own Pippenger MSM.

Legs composed:
  #490  field-mul : the in-Lean x86-backend CIOS Montgomery multiply
                    (bls12381_mont_mul_x86_from_lean) carries EVERY Fp mul/sqr
                    in the MSM (~1.07-1.09x blst_fp_mul).
  #488  inverse   : the AUCurves Bernstein-Yang safegcd divstep inverse
                    (bls12_invert_divstep_sat) carries the batched inversion
                    (~1.10x blst_fp_inverse).
  #481  batch-inv : Montgomery's trick in the affine-conversion path
                    (1 inverse + 3(n-1) muls instead of n inverses).

Composed MSM:
  - Pippenger bucketing with booth-style signed-digit window recoding
    (digits in [-2^(w-1), 2^(w-1)], carry low->high; negative digit adds the
    Y-negated affine point), balanced window w ~ floor(log2 n) - 2.
  - Jacobian G1 group law (add-2007-bl / dbl-2009-l / madd-2007-bl); every
    field multiply/square is the lean x86 kernel.  Fp add/sub use blst's so
    the composition is NOT advantaged on additions.

Oracle / KAT (NO fabricated vectors):
  blst_p1s_mult_pippenger over the SAME (points, scalars).  Points are real
  generator multiples (blst_p1_generator + blst_p1_mult); scalars a
  deterministic xorshift stream reduced mod r.  Result compared byte-identically
  on the 96-byte uncompressed serialisation.

KAT (gate):  composed-MSM == blst_p1s_mult_pippenger  at n = 256, 1024, 4096.
             batch-inversion: Montgomery's trick == per-point inversion.  PASS.

Microbench (this box, ~3.3 GHz; min-of-5, taskset -c 3):

  n      window  composed cyc/point   blst cyc/point   ratio   mul-eq/point
  256      6           61752.5           51339.3       1.203x      578.6
  1024     8           47377.3           38236.3       1.239x      435.4
  4096    10           38718.9           31212.1       1.241x      351.6

Verdict: 1.20-1.24x behind blst, near the field-mul (~1.08x) / safegcd-inverse
(~1.10x) modularity floor, with the per-point cost amortising as expected
(mul-eq/point drops 579 -> 352 as n grows -- Montgomery's trick + Pippenger).

Diagnosis of the residual (~0.1-0.15x above the field floor):
  - Per-field-op function-call overhead: Fp add/sub are external blst calls
    and the field-mul is an external leaf call per use, whereas blst inlines
    field ops and batches the bucket accumulation.  The ~500 add/sub per point
    in the generic Jacobian formulas pay call overhead the field-mul bench
    (#490) does not see.
  - Generic Jacobian add/double formula op-count vs blst's tuned formulas.
  This is a modularity gap (how the verified leaves are stitched), not a field
  arithmetic gap -- the field-mul and inverse legs are at parity-class to blst.

Note: a kani agent runs in faest-hax; ratios are min-of-5 so robust to
transient contention.
